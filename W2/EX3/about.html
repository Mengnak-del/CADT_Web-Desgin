This is about us
"WHO AM I?" was founded with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between high school graduation and higher education. We understand that choosing a future path can be overwhelming.
That is why we provide high school graduates with access to free , high-quality online learning resources, foundational skill workshops, and career discovery materials. Our platform helps students uncover their strengths, discover new passions, and enter university or the workforce with confidence.
